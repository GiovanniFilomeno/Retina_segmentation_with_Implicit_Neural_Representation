# Bibliography and reference material

This directory records literature that informed the exploratory project. Links below
point to publisher, conference, or author-hosted records so that readers can verify
the source and obtain the appropriate version. Inclusion does not imply endorsement
by the authors, publishers, datasets, or institutions.

## Core methods

1. Sitzmann, V., Martel, J. N. P., Bergman, A. W., Lindell, D. B., and Wetzstein, G.
   **Implicit Neural Representations with Periodic Activation Functions.** NeurIPS,
   2020. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.09661) ·
   [project](https://www.vincentsitzmann.com/siren/)
2. Tancik, M. et al. **Fourier Features Let Networks Learn High Frequency Functions
   in Low Dimensional Domains.** NeurIPS, 2020.
   [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10739) ·
   [project](https://bmild.github.io/fourfeat/)
3. Chen, Y., Liu, S., and Wang, X. **Learning Continuous Image Representation with
   Local Implicit Image Function.** CVPR, 2021.
   [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.09161) ·
   [upstream code](https://github.com/yinboc/liif)
4. Lin, T.-Y. et al. **Focal Loss for Dense Object Detection.** ICCV, 2017.
   [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.02002) ·
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2017.324)
5. Molaei, A. et al. **Implicit Neural Representation in Medical Imaging: A
   Comparative Survey.** ICCV Workshops, 2023.
   [Open-access paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023W/CVAMD/html/Molaei_Implicit_Neural_Representation_in_Medical_Imaging_A_Comparative_Survey_ICCVW_2023_paper.html) ·
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCVW60793.2023.00252)

## Retinal imaging and datasets

6. Jin, K. et al. **FIVES: A Fundus Image Dataset for Artificial Intelligence Based
   Vessel Segmentation.** *Scientific Data*, 2022.
   [Dataset](https://figshare.com/articles/figure/FIVES_A_Fundus_Image_Dataset_for_AI-based_Vessel_Segmentation/19688169) ·
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01564-3)
7. Hatamizadeh, A. et al. **RAVIR: A Dataset and Methodology for the Semantic
   Segmentation and Quantitative Analysis of Retinal Arteries and Veins in Infrared
   Reflectance Imaging.** *IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics*, 2022.
   [Dataset portal](https://ravir.grand-challenge.org/) ·
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/JBHI.2022.3163352)
8. Gu, J. et al. **Retinal vessel segmentation based on self-distillation and implicit
   neural representation.** *Applied Intelligence*, 2023.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10489-022-04252-2)
9. Qin, Q., and Chen, Y. **A review of retinal vessel segmentation for fundus image

   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2023.107454)
10. Ilesanmi, A. E. et al. **A systematic review of retinal fundus image segmentation
   and classification methods using convolutional neural networks.** *Healthcare
   Analytics*, 2023. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.health.2023.100261)
